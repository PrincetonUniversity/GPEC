    Perturbed field strength (pmodb) and gauge¶

    This page states what the columns of gpec_pmodb_n#.out are, how they are related, and which of them a field-strength consumer needs.

    Coordinates and displacement¶

    Assume smooth nested surfaces with B>0 and nonzero J, chi1 and q. Fourier inversions below additionally exclude nonzero resonant sources.

    GPEC works in the DCON chart \((\psi,\theta,\zeta)\) with angles in turns, any straight-field-line Jacobian \(\mathcal{J}(\psi,\theta)\) selected by jac_type, \(\mathcal{J}B^\theta=\chi_1\), \(\mathcal{J}B^\zeta=\chi_1 q\) and Fourier factor \(\exp[2\pi i(m\theta-n\zeta)]\). The Euler-Lagrange solution supplies \(\xi^\psi\), its radial derivative and the surface displacement (Glasser 2016, Eqs. 9 and 18)

    \[\xi_s = (\boldsymbol\xi\times\mathbf B)_\psi = \chi_1(q\xi^\theta-\xi^\zeta),\]

    the in-surface displacement across \(\mathbf B\) (xms; gpec_xclebsch writes \(\xi^\alpha=\xi_s/\chi_1\)). A field-aligned displacement changes neither \(\xi^\psi\) nor \(\xi_s\), so \((\xi^\psi,\xi_s)\) determine \(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\) completely. The parallel part used elsewhere in GPEC is fixed in gpeq_contra by \(\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi=0\), which gives xmt \(=\mathcal J\xi^\theta\) and xmz \(=\mathcal J\xi^\zeta\). The pmodb columns do not use xmt or xmz.

    For every jac_type the Jacobian-weighted perturbed field of gpeq_sol is \(\mathcal J\,\nabla\times(\boldsymbol\xi\times\mathbf B)\).

    The pmodb columns¶

    eulb

    \(\delta B_E=\hat{\mathbf b}\cdot\delta\mathbf B\), the Eulerian field strength.

    lagb

    \(\delta B_E+\xi^\psi\partial_\psi B+\xi_s/(\chi_1 q)\,\partial_\theta B\). This is the Lagrangian field strength of the displacement \(\boldsymbol\xi_g=\boldsymbol\xi_\perp+\lambda\mathbf B\) with \(\xi_g^\zeta=0\) in the working chart (jac_type). It is neither the \(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\)-only value nor Park et al. (2009) Eq. 7 with the divergence-free \(\boldsymbol\xi\) that GPEC constructs (that value is lagb \(+\,\xi^\zeta/q\,\partial_\theta B\)). jac_out only relabels the harmonics; it does not change this gauge.

    Bdivxprp

    \(B\,\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\). gpout_pmodb forms \(\mathcal J\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\) directly from \(\xi^\psi\) and \(\xi_s\),

    \[\mathcal J\xi_\perp^\theta=\chi_1^2\left(-f_2\,\xi^\psi+f_3\,\xi_s/\chi_1\right), \qquad \mathcal J\xi_\perp^\zeta=q\,\mathcal J\xi_\perp^\theta-\mathcal J\xi_s/\chi_1,\]

    with the equilibrium coefficients \(f_2=(g_{\psi\theta}+qg_{\psi\zeta})/(\mathcal JB^2)\) and \(f_3=(g_{\theta\zeta}+qg_{\zeta\zeta})/(\mathcal JB^2)\) (eqfun columns 2 and 3, covariant metric). The same projection gives the xmat-zmat matrices of idcon_build.

    Bkxprp

    \(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\cdot\nabla B+\mu_0p'\xi^\psi/B\). This is \(B\boldsymbol\kappa\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\) only through equilibrium force balance: exactly, \(B\boldsymbol\kappa\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp=\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\cdot\nabla B +\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\cdot[(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\times\mathbf B]/B\). The PENTRC matrices smat, tmat (idcon_build) form the same quantity times \(\mathcal J B^2\) from \(\xi^\psi\) and \(\xi^\alpha\).

    llagb (gpec_diagnostics_pmodb, pmodbmn_flag)

    \(\delta B_L(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp)=\delta B_E+\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\cdot\nabla B\) lllagb is \(-(\)Bdivxprp\(+\)Bkxprp\()\), and Bkxprp \(=\) llagb \(-\) eulb \(+\mu_0p'\xi^\psi/B\).

    Relations between the columns¶

    Gauge of lagb:

    \[\mathrm{lagb}=\delta B_L(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp)-\frac{\xi_\perp^\zeta}{q}\partial_\theta B, \qquad \xi_\perp^\zeta=-\frac{B_\theta\xi_s/\chi_1+qB_\psi\xi^\psi}{B_\theta+qB_\zeta}.\]

    Kinematic identity (any \(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\), no force balance) and its pmodb form:

    \[\delta B_L(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp)=-B(\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp +\boldsymbol\kappa\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp) =-(\mathrm{Bdivxprp}+\mathrm{Bkxprp})-\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\cdot\mathbf F/B, \qquad \mathbf F=(\nabla\times\mathbf B)\times\mathbf B-\mu_0\nabla p.\]

    Hence

    \[\mathrm{lagb}+\mathrm{Bdivxprp}+\mathrm{Bkxprp} =-\frac{\xi_\perp^\zeta}{q}\partial_\theta B-\frac{\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\cdot\mathbf F}{B}.\]

    lagb \(=-(\)Bdivxprp\(+\)Bkxprp\()\) is therefore not an identity. The first term is a gauge term of first order in the perturbation; its size depends on \(B_\theta/(qB_\zeta)\), \(B_\psi\), \(\partial_\theta B\) and the displacement spectrum. The second term vanishes for an exact equilibrium. In the output, finite Fourier truncation and spline error add to both.

    Regularisation¶

    With reg_flag (Park et al. 2009 Eq. 10), gpeq_sol replaces the radial derivative by \(R\,\partial_\psi\xi^\psi\), \(R=s^2/(s^2+\sigma^2)\), \(s=m-nq\), in the field and in xms. The regularised field is then not divergence free:

    \[\mathcal J\nabla\cdot\delta\mathbf B=2\pi i\chi_1 s(1-R)\,\partial_\psi\xi^\psi.\]

    Bdivxprp uses the same derivative xmp1 as the field, and \(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\) is built from the same xms, so the regularisation cancels between the columns and the relation above holds unchanged with the regularised quantities. Near rational surfaces the regularised field and displacement do not form an exact ideal-induction pair. This is a limitation of the smoothing policy, not evidence of resistive physics. With reg_flag=f the columns are unregularised and singular at rational surfaces.

    What consumers need¶

    PENTRC

    The drive of Logan et al. (2013) Eqs. 4-5 needs \(\delta B\) and \(\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi\) of the same displacement; the combination is then invariant under \(\boldsymbol\xi\to\boldsymbol\xi+\lambda\mathbf B\) up to a total derivative along the field line. Both PENTRC input paths use the \(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\) pair \(\delta B/B=-(\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp+\boldsymbol\kappa\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp)\) and \(\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\): the default path (peq_file = gpec_xclebsch, set_peq) from the smat-zmat matrices, and the pmodb_file path (read_pmodb) from \(-(\mathrm{Bdivxprp}+\mathrm{Bkxprp})\) and Bdivxprp. The lagb column is not read. Regenerate pmodb_file inputs with GPEC 1.6 or later.

    Bounce-averaged codes in perturbed Boozer or Hamada coordinates (NEO-RT, NEO-2, Shaing-type)

    need \(\delta B\) in the perturbed straight-field-line coordinates. That follows from \(\delta B_L(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp)\) (llagb, or \(-(\mathrm{Bdivxprp}+\mathrm{Bkxprp})\) away from rational surfaces) and \(\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_\perp\) by a magnetic differential equation for the parallel gauge (next section). lagb must not be used as \(\delta B_L(\boldsymbol\xi_\perp)\).

    Target gauges in GPEC units¶

    Let \(D=\partial_\theta+q\partial_\zeta\), so \(\mathbf B\cdot\nabla=(\chi_1/\mathcal J)D\). For a matching equilibrium chart \(\mathcal J B^a=H(\psi)\), \(a=2\) is Boozer and \(a=0\) is Hamada. A displacement with \(b_X=\delta B_L(\boldsymbol\xi_X)\) and \(d_X=\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi_X\) has target-gauge source

    \[T_a=d_X+a b_X/B,\qquad \boldsymbol\xi_{target}=\boldsymbol\xi_X+\lambda\mathbf B,\qquad T_a(\boldsymbol\xi_{target})=T_a(\boldsymbol\xi_X) +\frac{\mathbf B\cdot\nabla(\lambda B^a)}{B^a}.\]

    For nonaxisymmetric modes the target flux-function perturbation is zero. With \(w=\chi_1\lambda/\mathcal J\), this gives

    \[Dw=-T_a,\qquad w_m=\frac{i T_{a,m}}{2\pi(m-nq)},\qquad b_{target}=b_X+w\partial_\theta B.\]

    The source is formed in real space before Fourier transformation. A nonzero source at \(m=nq\) has no periodic solution. A resonant zero source leaves an arbitrary homogeneous shift, set to zero here. The factor \(2\pi\) is from angles in turns and cancels the same factor in \(\partial_\theta B\). Relabeling equilibrium harmonics with jac_out alone does not solve this equation.

    For a PEST chart \(\mathcal J/R^2\) is a flux function; its linear perturbation is \(\nabla\cdot\boldsymbol\xi-2\xi_R/R\). PEST output is not automatically either target gauge. Transform the equilibrium chart first, then solve the target equation.

    History¶

    Before GPEC 1.6.0, Bdivxprp and Bkxprp subtracted a Fourier-truncated parallel displacement, gpout_pmodb regularised the divergence even with reg_flag=f, gpdiag_pmodb used a stale Jacobian, PENTRC’s read_pmodb paired lagb with an inconsistent divergence, and both PENTRC CGL paths halved the curvature term. These were fixed in 1.6.0 (#340-#343).
